## Currently studying
### Mathematics
I am currently rebuilding my mathematical foundation for robotics.
Much of this is not entirely new to me — I learned many of these concepts before, but didn't retain them well. I am working through them again from the fundamentals, mostly using free courses available online. 
I started with MIT's 18.0x mathematics courses and am now exploring University of Michigan courses that connect the mathematics more directly to robotics.
### Robotics Core
I am working on building a deeper understanding of the core subjects in robotics, including state estimation, kinematics and dynamics, perception, SLAM, and motion planning.
My approach is to study these subjects from the mathematical foundations upward. Instead of only learning how an algorithm works or how to implement it, I want to understand the models, assumptions, and mathematics behind it, and how these ideas connect across different areas of robotics.
